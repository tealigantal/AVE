# Duration Blueprint System

Duration blueprints are flexible constraints, not templates and not simple
shortened versions of one another.

| Duration | Objective | Information density and beats | Pacing / hook / ending |
|---|---|---|---|
| 30s | One promise and payoff | 3–5 beats, almost no redundancy | Immediate evidence-backed hook; decisive ending |
| 60s | One compact arc | 4–7 beats, one turn and one emotional release | Fast opening, selective context, clean CTA or reflection |
| 2m | Coherent mini-story | 6–10 beats, room for setup and contrast | Pace can breathe at transitions; ending resolves the question |
| 5m | Character or process journey | 8–14 beats, recurring motif and meaningful setback | Alternating density; hook can be premise plus consequence |
| 10m | Layered narrative | Multiple sections, sub-goals and callbacks | Deliberate resets; redundancy only when it adds meaning |
| 30m | Documentary-scale experience | Chapters, evidence trails and long-form payoffs | Variable pacing, silence and context are acceptable; ending must integrate the thesis |

The planner records a duration budget, acceptable variance, beat density,
redundancy policy, emotional curve and ending contract. Material insufficiency
is surfaced rather than filled with invented events.
