# WO-RESEARCH-001 Video research pipeline

- Goal: ingest licensed references and produce reviewed Video Pattern Knowledge.
- Motivation: learn editing principles from excellent videos without popularity-only scraping.
- Input: permitted sources, metadata, visual/narrative analyzers and review policy.
- Output: provenance-rich observations, pattern records, counterexamples and expiration.
- Dependencies: WO-DOC-001.
- Non-goals: copyright-infringing copying, private-media upload by default, automatic trend control.
- Acceptance: rights, source, date, uncertainty and observation/inference separation are mandatory.
