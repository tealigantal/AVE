# Product Strategy

## Product goal

Help Vlog creators turn real media into a publishable, traceable result through
conversation and review, without sacrificing creator control, recoverability,
or media correctness.

## Core users

- creators who want AI speed but retain story and identity control;
- solo or small-team creators who cannot spend every session repairing basic
  Timeline or media errors;
- advanced creators who need inspectable evidence, versions, and execution
  details when a result matters.

## User value

AVE reduces the cost of understanding material, exploring story directions,
assembling a first cut, diagnosing feedback, and applying local revisions. It
must also reduce risk: no silent omission, no proxy-as-Master, no unexplained
creative decision, and no lost committed version.

## Product boundaries

AVE is a co-editor, not an autonomous publisher. The user owns goals,
representation, sensitive choices, final story acceptance, and delivery.
Project Host owns project-state validation and commit authority. Models and
Workers produce candidates only.

## MVP and programme boundary

The accepted P0 baseline covers the reliable media loop: project state,
versioned Timeline, Preview/Master rendering, QC, and recovery. The currently
implemented or blocked boundary is not inferred here; use
[`docs/current/STATUS.md`](../current/STATUS.md).

The next product milestone is not “all intelligence documents exist.” It is a
real user journey in which evidence-bound creative proposals can reach the
existing execution path and survive rejection, approval, render, QC, reopen,
and revision without authority drift.

## Strategic principles

1. Reliability before spectacle.
2. Evidence before narrative assertion.
3. Alternatives before irreversible action.
4. Local, scoped patches before full regeneration.
5. Creator identity before trends or generic optimization.
6. Real-media and human acceptance before capability claims.
7. Explicit blockers before approximate success.

## Non-goals

AVE does not optimize primarily for content volume, engagement prediction,
template count, or maximum automation. Shared catalogs, cloud learning,
Marketplace execution, and automatic publication require separate product,
privacy, security, and architecture decisions.
