# Viral Video Pattern Database

The database stores reusable observations rather than recipes. A record has
`pattern_id`, version, claim, observed evidence, context, examples,
counterexamples, risk, confidence, provenance, rights and review status.

Example: “Fast emotional hook” may be observed in successful travel videos when
the first three seconds contain a conflict or question. It may apply to a
personal vlog, but risks undermining documentary authenticity. It therefore
becomes a candidate recommendation, not a mandatory opening.
