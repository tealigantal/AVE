# Product Metrics

Metrics are evaluation signals, never automatic permission to publish.

## Story and editing quality

- Story: completeness, causal coherence, character continuity, beat evidence
  coverage, emotional curve and factual fidelity.
- Editing: shot suitability, pacing against the chosen duration blueprint,
  dialogue intelligibility, music relationship, continuity, transitions and
  absence of unexplained omissions.
- Intelligence: decision explanation completeness, evidence precision,
  calibrated confidence, alternative usefulness and blocker correctness.

## Product and technical quality

- Product: acceptance rate, revision count, time to first useful cut, patch
  locality, user override rate and approval latency.
- Technical: valid `CommandEditIR`, atomic CommitPlan, version conflict safety,
  Preview/Master semantic equivalence, render success and QC pass rate.

Metrics must be segmented by duration, content type, creator experience and
human-reviewed versus synthetic evaluation. Retention or engagement data is
optional research evidence and must never become a proxy for creator intent.
