# Creative Workflow

## Creative Contract

The contract records creator voice, audience, purpose, platform, target
duration, factual boundaries, protected material, sensitivity constraints,
reference intent and acceptance criteria. It is a user-owned, versioned input;
the model may suggest clarifications but may not silently rewrite it.

## Direction to delivery

1. Material understanding produces evidence, not a story.
2. Skills select decisions that are justified by evidence and context.
3. Story generation proposes alternatives and binds beats to source moments.
4. Editing reasoning converts accepted beats into Edit Intent with local effects.
5. Host validates, simulates and commits ordinary Timeline Commands.
6. RenderGraph and QC expose technical and semantic results for review.

The creative layer may choose among valid alternatives, but unsupported
capabilities must remain visible as a blocker or an explicitly approved
fallback. Aesthetic confidence never overrides source identity or timeline
version checks.
